Tips for a useful report

  • 1Step back so the photo shows the whole area, plus something recognisable like a bench, sign or tree.
  • 2Use “Use my location” while you're standing at the spot. It makes the cleanup easier to verify.
  • 3Be honest about the size. It decides how many points the cleanup is worth.
Stay safe. Don't go onto private property, roads or unstable riverbanks to take a photo.

What happens next

  • Reported
    Your spot goes on the map, and you get +10 pts.
  • Claimed
    You or another volunteer commits to clean it.
  • Cleaned
    An “after” photo is added and the cleaner earns points.
