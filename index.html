How it works

Three steps from “someone should” to done.

You don't need a team, a permit or a plan. Reporting takes under a minute. Cleaning is up to you, and so is what you do with the points.

01

Snap and report

Take a photo of the polluted spot, pin the location and say roughly how much waste is there. It goes on the shared map for anyone nearby to see.

+10 pts per report
02

Clean it, if you want

Claim any open spot, yours or someone else's. When you're done, add an “after” photo from the same place so the community can see the difference.

Optional
03

Earn and redeem

Verified cleanups earn points based on how much you cleared. Cash them out to your account or send them to a friend, family member or local cause.

50–250 pts per cleanup
Proof, not promises

Every point is backed by a before and after.

Points only go to real work. Each cleanup is checked against the original report before anything is paid out.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still have one? Write to us at hello@greenstep.app.

Do I have to clean a spot to report it?

No. Reporting is useful by itself. It shows volunteers and local services where help is needed. You earn 10 points for each new spot you report.

How are cleanups verified?

Your “after” photo is compared with the original report: same place, waste removed. Other users nearby can confirm it or flag it. Points become withdrawable once the cleanup passes review.

What can I do with my points?

Cash them out at 1,000 points = $5.00 (minimum 1,000 points), or gift any amount to another person. Gifts arrive in their Green Step wallet right away.

What should I bring to a cleanup?

Gloves, sturdy bags and closed shoes cover most jobs. Don't touch sharp objects, chemicals or anything that looks dangerous. Mark the spot as hazardous instead.

Where does the reward money come from?

Rewards are paid for by partners: local councils, businesses and sponsors who want cleaner neighbourhoods and pay for verified results, not promises.
